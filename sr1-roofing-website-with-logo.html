Every roof is different. An inspection can help you decide what makes sense for your property.

Ask a Question ↗
Do I always need a full roof replacement if my roof leaks?

Not always. A leak may come from a specific damaged area or flashing issue, but only a proper assessment can determine whether a repair or replacement is appropriate.

What should I do after a storm?

From a safe location, look for visible signs such as missing shingles or water stains indoors. Avoid climbing onto the roof. Contact a roofing professional to assess possible damage.

How long does a roofing project take?

Timing depends on roof size, materials, weather, access, and the scope of work. Ask for a project-specific schedule during the estimate.

Can I get an estimate before deciding?

Contact SR1 Roofing to ask about the current inspection and estimate process, and what information is needed for your property.
